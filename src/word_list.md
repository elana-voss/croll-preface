# Word List

The words the Preface uses in a sense of its own or returns to often, in today's spelling, with the book's own spellings. Each sense is marked: *plain* where the word means what it ordinarily means, *veiled* where the text uses it in a covered or second sense; *Stated* where the text itself says the meaning, *Interpreted* where the meaning is our reading of the lines given, *several* where more than one reading has its basis; and whose words they are (`CROLL`, the author; `QUOTED: <name>`, a voice he reports; `UNCLEAR`). *note-only* marks a sense that rests on a margin note alone, *wording-dependent* one that rests on a single English word of the translator's. Each quote is followed by the reference of the line it stands in.

1. Men who contemplate and admire the workmanship of Nature, the heavens and sublunary bodies, and come to understand and imitate its secrets. That these are the genuine kind is inferred from CH5.59 calling some “also false and lying Theophrasteans” (CH5.59). *(plain · Interpreted · voice: CROLL)*

   - “As for the spurious and adulterous Theophrasteans (the worst and wickedest sort of men)” [OPEN.11]
   - “carry their cheating impostures up and downe the Country” [OPEN.11]
   - “who under disguised names vaunt and vapour that they make use of Theophrastus his medicines” [CH5.59]
   - “these circumforaneous and adulterous Theophrasteans, with their unlawfully prepared Remedies” [CH5.60]
   - “wherewith they put the sick in danger of death by doubtfull and uncertaine experiments” [CH5.60]
