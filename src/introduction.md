# Introduction

Oswald Croll (the book spells him Crollie) wrote an *Admonitory Preface* to his work on chemical physic. In 1657 Henry Pinnell printed it in English, at the head of *Philosophy Reformed & Improved in Four Profound Tractates* (London). This site reads that Preface, from its title to the end of its sixth chapter, and says in plain words what it teaches. The Paracelsus treatises later in the volume, and Pinnell's own front matter, are not covered.

**The book is the only source.** Everything here was written from the English text of 1657 alone, with no outside knowledge about Croll, Paracelsus or alchemy. Quotes keep the book's spelling.

**References.** Every statement points to the text. `[CH2.17]` is paragraph 17 of Chapter II; `[OPEN.24]` is paragraph 24 of the opening address to the reader; `[MICRO.5]` is paragraph 5 of the passage on the Microcosm, the Little World Man; paragraph 0 is a section's head; `[CH4.9–10]` is paragraphs 9 to 10. `[N003]` is the third margin note, counted through the whole Preface. The numbering is that of `text/preface.json` in this site's repository, made by script from the transcription below.

**What is here**

- One page per section of the Preface, as the book divides it: the opening address, Chapters I to VI, and the passage on the Microcosm between Chapters I and II.
- *The Preface as a Whole*: its argument in one page.
- The two subjects the Preface carries, the True Physic and the one chief Medicine of the most ancient Philosophers, each on two pages: *in Plain Terms*, with the passages behind every point, and *in Plain Language*, the same in everyday words without references.
- A word list: the words the Preface uses in a sense of its own, with the book's spellings and each sense quoted and referenced.
