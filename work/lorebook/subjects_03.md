## Artist

The book spells it: Artist, Artists

1. The skilled worker in Chymicall employments, a master of his craft, whom the preparations require. *(veiled · Stated · voice: CROLL)*
   - “these preparations doe require such an Artist, and not one of a froward and sottish disposition” [OPEN.7]
   - “To the Artists aforesaid (I say) who are their Crafts-masters” [OPEN.7]
   - “the labour & paines of the Artist” [N011]
   - “no industry or skill of Artist or Nature can perpetuate the wedlock of life and death” [CH5.68]
2. The worker in the art, who must agree with God the first cause, moved by him to understanding. *(veiled · Stated · voice: QUOTED: Lullius)*
   - “between the Artist and God the first cause, there ought to be an agreement without contrariety” [CH6.44]
   - “should move the Intelligence or Soule of the Artist to a true Understanding” [CH6.44]
3. God, the perfect maker of all things. *(veiled · Stated · voice: CROLL)*
   - “the perfect Artist of all things, even the most high Creatour and glorious God” [CH1.1]
4. A name given to Elias, who is to come in the third age and restore all things. My inference: the name marks him as the one who restores. *(veiled · Interpreted · voice: CROLL)*
   - “Elias the Artist” [OPEN.10]
   - “who is to restore all things, shall come” [OPEN.10]
