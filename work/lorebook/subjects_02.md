# SUBJECT: specific

## Specific

The book spells it: Specifick, specifick

1. Noun: what the one Nature is to every creature; the narrowest reading is that which gives each creature its own kind. *(veiled · Interpreted · voice: CROLL)*
   - “which Nature is the Specifick of every Creature” [CH3.30]
2. Of a virtue: a thing's own inward power, set against its outward qualities, by which it cures. *(plain · Stated · voice: CROLL)*
   - “not (as I have said) by their Externall superficiary qualities, but by an Internall, proper, specifick, harmonious, similer virtue” [CH2.7]
   - “by the Specifick venemous vertue of the Opium which hath a greater degree of poyson” [CH4.6]
   - “by a specifick and Harmonicall vertue serve to cure and comfort the heart” [CH2.5]
