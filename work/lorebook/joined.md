
## Microcosm

The book spells it: Microcosm, MICROCOSM, Microcosme, microcosm

1. Man as the little world, into whom the whole great world is gathered, answering to the Macrocosm as child to parent *(veiled · Stated · voice: CROLL)*

   - “the Microcosm the child ought always to answer to the fourfold members of the Macrocosm its parent” [CH1.12]
   - “the Nature, as well of the Macrocosme as the Microcosme, is its own medicine, disease, and Physitian” [CH1.6]
   - “reduce the whole Microcosm into a true Temperament” [CH6.38]

   - “the prudent Ancients wilsely called Man a Microcosm or little World” [MICRO.14]
3. Man as holding every particular of the Universe *(veiled · Stated · voice: QUOTED: Interpreters of Secret Nature)*

4. the word set beside the outer world: the externall Elements “are the very Microcosm” (CH1.6), and man's Parent is called “the externall Heart of the Microcosm” (CH2.4). Readings: (1) the outer world or its Elements are themselves called Microcosm, because they are like man (basis: “for they are alike” (CH1.6); “his Parent or” (CH2.4)); (2) the word still names man, and the outer things are tied to him as his likeness: in CH1.6 “they” may be the Elements and Man together, in CH2.4 the externall Heart is the one that answers to man's (basis: “Man also is understood” (CH1.6); “which is one like the other” (CH2.4)) *(veiled · several · voice: CROLL · wording-dependent)*

   - “his Parent or the externall Heart of the Microcosm which is one like the other” [CH2.4]
