# SUBJECT: limus terrae

## Limus Terrae

The book spells it: Limus Terrae, Limo Terrae, LIMO TERRAE, limo terrae

1. The slime, clay or dust of the earth out of which man was taken, which the text explains as the frame of the whole world, the great world, man's mortal father; it is two-fold, visible and invisible, and called a hidden secret thing. *(veiled · Stated · voice: CROLL)*
   - “drawn or taken (è limo terrae) out of the slime of the Earth or visible frame of the whole consisting of Heaven or Heavenly Sphaeres and the Earth” [MICRO.11]
   - “Man was created è Limo Terrae, out of the clay or dust of the Earth” [MICRO.22]
   - “ex LIMO TERRAE, out of the dust of the ground or great world as to the mortall Body which is visible and invisible” [MICRO.31]
   - “a Mortall one, which is the whole world with all the creatures, that is, that Limus Terrae, that slime of the earth, or hidden Secret thing” [MICRO.17]
   - “The Limus Terrae, or dust of the Earth, is two-fold, visible and invisible” [MICRO.23]
2. Man himself, named with the Microcosm, as made up of all bodies and created things. *(veiled · Stated · voice: CROLL)*
   - “the Microcosm or Limus Terrae, Man is compacted of all bodies and created things” [MICRO.12]
3. The earthly mind, set against the image or inspiration of the most High, after which the friends of God do not live. *(veiled · Stated · voice: CROLL)*
   - “who live after the Image or inspiration of the most High, and not after the Limus Terrae the Eearthy Mind” [CH6.68]
