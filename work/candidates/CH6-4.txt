CH6.76 | concealment | CROLL | “after other secret Sciences which yet lie in the dark” | secret Sciences | Other secret Sciences are said still to lie in the dark.
CH6.76 | marking | CROLL | “that Ancient, True, & Philosophical Medicin, which by reason of the long continuance and injury of time, as also the unworthines of our age (mens sins doubtles so meeting together) is not yet fully known” | that Ancient, True, & Philosophical Medicin | The Medicine is named once more at the close, Ancient, True and Philosophical, and said to be not yet fully known through the injury of time and the unworthiness of the age.
CH6.76 | position | CROLL | “may in a shorttime by the heavenly assistance, be restored to its lost lustre & ancient splendor” | He expects the Medicine may soon, by heavenly help, be restored to its ancient splendour, for the good of mankind and the honour of Spagyrick Phisitians.
CH6.76 | position | CROLL | “whose endeavor & pains that immense sea of divine Mercy would be pleased to make use of as an Instrument & Pen to accomplish so healthful and saving a work” | The Spagyrick physicians' pains are wished to be God's Instrument and Pen for this work.
CH6.76 | internal conflict | CROLL | “it concerns the publick good to make known also in publick writing what belongs to the publick” | Here what belongs to the public is to be made known in public writing; the same paragraph says some things are left obscure by the Philosophical law and that he imparted only so far as was lawful, and CH6.71 says the most secret of secrets have ever been hid from the common sort. The lines given do not show where the line between the two is drawn.
CH6.77 | position | CROLL | “Which that holy Triunity grant, whose unspeakable Name be blessed for ever and ever” | Triunity | Closing prayer that the holy Triunity grant what the last paragraph hopes for.
CH6.78 | marking | CROLL | “AMEN” | AMEN | The last word of the text given; the Preface ends.
