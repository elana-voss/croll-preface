# Task: the Preface as a whole

Your bundle has no book text. It holds the checked pages that set out the Preface section by section, each labelled `SECTION PAGE` with its section id, and `DIVISION`, the settled answer on whether the book marks a process, with its basis, and the units of what it carries, each with its id, name and kind. These are your only sources: add nothing, and confirm nothing elsewhere.

Write one page that lets a reader take in the whole Preface: what it is and how it is built, what it argues in its own order, what it carries, how openly it says it, and what it leaves open.

## How to write
- Every statement ends with a reference bracket copied from the page that makes it, naming the lines as that page names them, never a whole section. Note the references with each point as you read; your first reading will not stay with you to the end.
- A quote is copied from a page word for word, in curly quotation marks, with its reference after it in the same paragraph. Quote sparingly. No straight quotation marks (`"`).
- Keep each point's standing as its page gives it: the author's own, a view he reports or rejects, a margin note, or the page's reading. A point a page gives as an inference stays one and opens with `Our reading is`; several readings stay several; what a page leaves open stays open.
- Where two pages disagree, give both and say that they do.
- Plain, connected prose for a reader new to the book. Keep the book's own words for its things and say what the pages say they are or do.
- A unit is named by its name in `DIVISION`, in today's spelling, and linked once to its two pages: `[<name> in Plain Terms](units/<id>.md)` and `[<name> in Plain Language](units/<id>_plain.md)`.

## The page
`# The Preface as a Whole`

Then these sections:
- `## What the Preface is`: who writes it to whom, what it sets out to do, and its sections in order, one sentence each.
- `## The argument in its own order`: a paragraph or two per section, in the book's order, under the section's name.
- `## Process or not, and what the Preface carries`: the division's answer, given as the finding of the division made from the readings, never as something the book says of itself; then one paragraph per unit: what it is, where it stands, what it is said to do, with its two links.
- `## What is covered, and how`: what the pages find about silence, covered speech and to whom things are given.
- `## What the Preface leaves open`: a list of the main open questions.

Length: about 3,000 words; do not repeat the section pages' detail.

Write the page to the file your task names, ending with the closing lines. Then reply with one line: the file written and its word count.
