# For the page on the Preface as a whole

The page draws on checked section pages you are not given; your bundle gives the book's lines it cites. Besides the checks above:
- Each statement ends with a reference bracket that names its lines; a statement whose reference does not bear it out is narrowed to what the lines say, or removed.
- Points that open with `Our reading is` stay readings; several readings stay several; what is open stays open.
- The page's form stays: its headings as given, in their order, and the unit links `[<name> in Plain Terms](units/<id>.md)` and `[<name> in Plain Language](units/<id>_plain.md)`.
