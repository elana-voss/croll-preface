# For a Plain Language page

The page retells the page labelled `CHECKED PAGE` in everyday words. Besides the checks above:
- Every point of the checked page is there, its answer on process and its open questions included; nothing is added that the checked page does not hold.
- No quotes from the book, no straight or curly quotation marks around the book's words.
- Each word an ordinary reader would not know is replaced by what the book says the thing is or does.
- Interpreted points open with `Our reading is`; several readings stay several; what is open stays open.
- A reference bracket ends each paragraph and list item, and no sentence mentions one.
- The opening's pointer to the checked page and the last sub-heading, `## What the book leaves open`, are the page's form: keep them.
