# Task: check and correct a written page

Your bundle holds the book's lines the page draws on, the page labelled `PAGE`, and the findings of the checking scripts labelled `SCRIPT FINDINGS`. A thing missing from your bundle may still stand elsewhere in the book.

## Check every claim against the text
- Each quote: word for word at its reference? The scripts list the quotes they could not find, the references that do not exist, and the words of the page found neither in the book nor in plain English. Fix each: the right reference, the exact words, or remove the claim that rests on it.
- Each claim: does the cited text say it? Narrow a claim that says more than the text, and remove one the text does not bear out.
- Voices: words given to someone else are not the author's.
- Readings of covered speech: marked Stated, Interpreted or several readings, and Stated only where the text itself says the meaning.
- Words from outside the book: replace with the book's own word or plain words, or remove.
- No claim that the book never says something, unless a search result in your bundle shows it, and no remark on what the text does not say or explain or on the lines given: remove it.
- Curly quotation marks only; a reference bracket after each quote, in the same paragraph.

Do not add new material, except where a correction needs a quote or a reference.

## Output
Write the corrected page, whole, to the file your task names, ending with the closing lines. Then reply with a short list of what you changed and why, one line each, and nothing else.
