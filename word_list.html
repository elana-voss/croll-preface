Croll's Admonitory Preface — A Reading

Word List

The words the Preface uses in a sense of its own or returns to often, in today’s spelling, with the book’s own spellings. Each sense is marked: plain where the word means what it ordinarily means, veiled where the text uses it in a covered or second sense; Stated where the text itself says the meaning, Interpreted where the meaning is our reading of the lines given, several where more than one reading has its basis; and whose words they are (CROLL, the author; QUOTED: <name>, a voice he reports; UNCLEAR). note-only marks a sense that rests on a margin note alone, wording-dependent one that rests on a single English word of the translator’s. Each quote is followed by the reference of the line it stands in.
